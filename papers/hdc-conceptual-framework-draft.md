# Hierarchical Decoherence Cosmology
## Conceptual Framework Draft

**Framework Name:** Hierarchical Decoherence Cosmology (HDC)  
**Core Claim:** Classicality may fail to propagate globally across cosmological horizons.  
**Current Status:** Interpretational quantum-information framework ready for literature mapping and toy-model construction.

---

# 1. Current Position of the Framework

Hierarchical Decoherence Cosmology (HDC) is a speculative framework positioned near:

- quantum foundations,
- decoherence theory,
- Quantum Darwinism,
- black hole information theory,
- holography,
- emergent spacetime,
- and quantum cosmology.

It is not currently proposed as:

- a completed theory of quantum gravity,
- a predictive cosmological model,
- or a mathematically established theory of everything.

Instead, HDC isolates a narrower and more precise question:

> Can classicality be hierarchy-local rather than globally transitive?

More specifically:

> Can a horizon-bounded subsystem become internally classical without inducing a corresponding externally classicalized history basis in the parent frame?

This is now the framework's central question.

The project has evolved away from speculative cosmological narrative and toward a focused quantum-information problem involving:

- decoherence,
- classical records,
- redundancy,
- scrambling,
- and horizon-restricted information flow.

The remaining work is now primarily technical rather than conceptual.

---

# 2. The Central Thesis

## Decoherence may be non-transitive across cosmological horizons.

Standard decoherence theory explains how effectively classical behavior emerges when quantum systems interact with environments.

Most cosmological interpretations implicitly assume that once decoherence occurs, the resulting classicality becomes effectively universal.

HDC questions that assumption.

The proposal is that:

- internal decoherence may occur,
- internal pointer states may stabilize,
- internal observers may experience ordinary classical spacetime,
- while external observer frames fail to stabilize one corresponding classical macroscopic history.

In this framework, a cosmological horizon is interpreted not merely as a causal boundary, but potentially as a restriction on the upward propagation of stable classical records.

---

# 3. Internal Classicality vs External Classicalization

This distinction is now the conceptual center of HDC.

Internal subsystems may support:

- memory,
- causality,
- entropy growth,
- irreversible histories,
- stable pointer states,
- and effective classical spacetime.

However, this does not automatically imply that the parent observer frame externally classicalizes the subsystem into one unique macroscopic history.

Thus:

> Internal classicality does not necessarily imply external classicalization.

The framework therefore treats classicality as potentially hierarchy-local rather than globally absolute.

---

# 4. The Most Important Distinction

The strongest conceptual refinement of HDC is now the distinction between:

```text
Quantum correlations
!=
Accessible stable records
!=
Redundant environmental record proliferation
!=
Externally classicalized history
```

These are related but non-equivalent structures.

This hierarchy is central to the framework.

Modern black hole information theory increasingly suggests that information may survive through subtle quantum correlations encoded in:

- Hawking radiation,
- holography,
- Page-curve behavior,
- island reconstruction,
- or scrambling dynamics.

HDC does not reject that possibility.

Instead, the framework asks:

> Does recoverable quantum information necessarily imply externally accessible classical history selection?

The proposed answer is:

> Not necessarily.

A parent frame may preserve or recover quantum correlations without receiving:

- stable pointer-basis records,
- redundant environmental encoding,
- or externally classicalized macroscopic history structure.

This distinction may allow HDC to remain compatible with:

- unitarity,
- Page-curve consistency,
- holographic encoding,
- quantum-information preservation,
- and black hole complementarity-style ideas,

without requiring:

- information destruction,
- perfectly thermal featureless radiation,
- or explicit non-unitary physics.

---

# 5. Quantum Darwinism and Record Redundancy

Quantum Darwinism may provide the most useful formal language for sharpening HDC.

In Quantum Darwinism, classical objectivity emerges because information about a system becomes redundantly encoded across many fragments of the environment.

Observers agree on a classical world because stable pointer-state information proliferates redundantly throughout the environment.

HDC does not reduce to Quantum Darwinism.

However, Quantum Darwinism clarifies the exact structure HDC is attempting to isolate.

The key question becomes:

> What happens if a horizon suppresses or scrambles the redundant environmental proliferation required for external classical objectivity?

Under this interpretation:

- internal decoherence may still occur,
- internal records may still stabilize,
- internal observers may still experience classical reality,

while the external parent frame receives only:

- sparse,
- scrambled,
- non-redundant,
- reconstruction-dependent quantum correlations.

This would allow:

> Internal classical records without externally classicalized history selection.

That possibility is now one of the framework's strongest directions.

---

# 6. Why Ordinary Hidden Systems Are Insufficient

HDC must distinguish cosmological horizons from ordinary environmental inaccessibility.

Ordinary opaque systems already permit:

- hidden dynamics,
- inaccessible information,
- and local decoherence.

So why would horizons be different?

The proposed distinction is not simple opacity.

It is informational compression combined with restricted record propagation.

Externally, black holes appear through dramatically compressed macroscopic descriptions:

- mass,
- charge,
- angular momentum,
- entropy,
- temperature,
- and radiation structure.

Meanwhile, the possible internal Hilbert space may be enormously richer.

Thus:

> The parent frame may remain compatible with quantum information recovery while still lacking sufficient redundant record structure to externally classicalize one unique macroscopic history.

This is stronger than merely saying:
> "the parent cannot see inside."

The proposal is instead:

> The parent frame may fundamentally fail to receive the type of redundant pointer-basis encoding required for external classicalization.

---

# 7. Scrambling and the Horizon Channel

The horizon should not be modeled as a simple wall.

A more useful intuition is:

> The horizon acts as a restricted scrambling channel.

Information may pass outward in encoded or unitary form without preserving externally stable classical record structure.

This allows HDC to avoid two extremes:

1. Complete information destruction
2. Fully exported external classicalization

Instead, the framework proposes:

```text
Quantum correlations may survive
without stable redundant classical-record propagation.
```

This distinction may become especially important for interpreting Hawking radiation.

Even if Hawking radiation eventually encodes information about the subsystem, the encoding may remain:

- highly scrambled,
- nonlocal,
- reconstruction-dependent,
- and not equivalent to stable macroscopic classical records.

---

# 8. The Everettian Branching Problem

The largest remaining conceptual issue in HDC is now clear.

The framework must avoid collapsing into ordinary Everettian branching.

The danger is:

> The parent frame may simply branch together with the internal subsystem through entanglement.

If that occurs generically, HDC loses distinctiveness.

The emerging HDC response is:

> Entanglement alone may not be sufficient for parent-frame classical branching.

More specifically:

> The parent frame may become entangled with the subsystem without receiving enough redundant structured information to stabilize a corresponding external pointer basis.

This is the critical distinction.

Under HDC:

- the subsystem may decohere internally,
- internal pointer states may stabilize,
- internal classical records may emerge,

while the parent frame receives only:

- sparse,
- scrambled,
- non-redundant quantum correlations insufficient for externally classicalized history selection.

Thus:

```text
Entanglement
!=
Externally classicalized branching
```

or at minimum:

> not automatically.

Whether this distinction survives formalization is now one of the central mathematical questions of the framework.

# 9. Relationship to Existing Physics

HDC exists near several established areas of modern theoretical physics.

---

## 9.1 Many-Worlds Interpretation

Many-Worlds rejects global collapse and permits branching structure.

HDC differs by asking whether classical realization itself may be hierarchy-dependent.

To remain distinct from ordinary Everettian branching, HDC must show that:

> Internal classicalization does not necessarily induce corresponding external classical branching.

This remains the central unresolved issue.

---

## 9.2 Relational Quantum Mechanics

Relational Quantum Mechanics treats quantum states as observer-relative.

HDC extends this relational structure to cosmological hierarchy itself.

It asks whether an entire horizon-bounded subsystem may become classically real relative to internal observers while remaining unresolved relative to the parent frame.

---

## 9.3 Quantum Darwinism

Quantum Darwinism provides a useful language for distinguishing:

- decoherence,
- record redundancy,
- environmental proliferation,
- and classical objectivity.

HDC increasingly appears to hinge on whether horizons suppress the redundant propagation required for parent-frame classical objectivity.

---

## 9.4 Holography and emergent spacetime

Modern quantum gravity increasingly suggests:

- spacetime emerges from entanglement structure,
- geometry may not be fundamental,
- and horizons possess deep informational significance.

If spacetime itself emerges from informational structure, then the question:

> Is classical spacetime globally transitive?

may become physically meaningful rather than merely philosophical.

Recent work involving:

- scrambling,
- island formulas,
- entanglement wedge reconstruction,
- holographic quantum error correction,
- and black hole information recovery

may already contain structures partially compatible with HDC's central intuition.

HDC may therefore become:

- an interpretational synthesis,
- a partially overlapping framework,
- or ultimately reducible to existing structures.

All remain possible.

# 10. The Toy Model Path

The next meaningful step is no longer conceptual expansion.

It is technical formalization.

The goal is not to reproduce full quantum gravity.

The goal is to test the core information-theoretic mechanism.

---

## 10.1 Minimal structure

### Parent system

```text
P
```

### Horizon-bounded subsystem

```text
B
```

### Internal environment

```text
E_B
```

### Restricted scrambling channel

```text
H
```

Desired behavior:

- B decoheres relative to E_B.
- Internal pointer states stabilize.
- Internal records become effectively classical.
- P accesses B only through H.
- H transmits scrambled quantum correlations rather than redundant classical records.
- P fails to stabilize one externally classicalized history basis for B.

---

## 10.2 Critical requirement

The toy model must NOT merely hide information.

The model must demonstrate that:

- information still propagates outward,
- correlations still survive,
- unitarity may still hold,

while simultaneously failing to produce:

- redundant pointer-basis encoding,
- stable external classical records,
- or externally classicalized history selection.

This distinction is essential.

Otherwise the model collapses into ordinary opacity rather than non-transitive classicalization.

---

## 10.3 Central mathematical target

Can one construct a Hamiltonian or quantum channel such that:

```text
rho_B
```

becomes approximately diagonal in an internal pointer basis,

while:

```text
rho_P
```

fails to become diagonal in any basis corresponding to distinct macroscopic histories of B?

This is now the framework's core mathematical problem.

---

## 10.4 Possible toy construction

One possible starting point:

- B = small spin-chain or few-qubit subsystem
- E_B = internal decohering environment
- H = restricted scrambling channel
- P = external parent frame

Schematic:

```text
B  <-->  E_B

B  -->  H  -->  P
```

Internal decoherence stabilizes local classical records.

But the horizon channel fails to redundantly export those records into the parent environment.

The success condition becomes:

> Internal classicality without external classical history stabilization.

This would not prove HDC as cosmology.

But it would demonstrate that the core behavior is possible in principle.

# 11. Criteria for Failure

HDC likely fails if any of the following become unavoidable:

- decoherence generically propagates upward through horizon-like channels,
- quantum information recovery necessarily implies external classical history selection,
- parent-frame branching inevitably mirrors internal branching,
- the framework collapses into standard Many-Worlds without additional structure,
- or no meaningful distinction between quantum correlations and redundant classical record propagation can be formalized.

A speculative framework becomes more scientifically useful when it clearly defines what would count against it.

---

# 12. Criteria for Success

HDC becomes substantially stronger if it can achieve any of the following:

- a successful toy-model demonstration,
- rigorous formalization of non-transitive classicalization,
- clear separation between quantum recoverability and classical record propagation,
- compatibility with Page-curve unitarity,
- meaningful distinction from ordinary Everettian branching,
- or useful reinterpretation of horizon information structure.

Predictive power would eventually be required for the framework to become physics rather than interpretation.

But toy-model and interpretational consistency are the current thresholds.

# 14. Final Summary

The strongest current form of Hierarchical Decoherence Cosmology is:

> Horizons may preserve quantum correlations while preventing the redundant propagation of stable classical records required for external classicalization.

This formulation:

- sharpens the framework,
- connects it to Quantum Darwinism,
- distinguishes it from ordinary hidden systems,
- clarifies compatibility with modern black hole information theory,
- and gives the toy model a concrete mathematical target.

The central unresolved question is now:

> Can internal classicality emerge without inducing externally classicalized history selection?

If yes, HDC gains a meaningful foothold in quantum-information foundations.

If no, the framework likely collapses into ordinary Everettian branching or standard environmental inaccessibility.

Either result would still be useful.

That means the framework has now reached the correct stage:

> narrow enough to fail,
> precise enough to formalize,
> and focused enough to begin technical investigation.
